Creamy Blackened Chicken Ramen

Description

Chicken is a wonderfully tasty food! Ramen is also a scrumptious choice!

The edible parts of this dish will give you a lot of energy after you eat them!

Do not try to eat the bowl! That part is certainly inedible!

Serves 2

  • Calories: 768
  • Fat: 44g
  • Carbs: 55g
  • Protein: 38g

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 skinless, boneless chicken breast
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Broth

  • 1 1/2 cloves garlic, minced or equivalent paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili flakes
  • 2 tablespoons marinara sauce
  • 3/4 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 1/4 cups chicken stock, divided
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 packages ramen noodles
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 sliced green onion
  • cilantro leaves
