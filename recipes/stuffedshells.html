Stuffed Shells

Description

So many shells! Wow! I promise we are not in the ocean!

These shells contain many flavors, including good ones! Please taste them.

You may be disappointed, but the probability is very low!

Serves 14

  • Calories: 323
  • Fat: 11g
  • Carbs: 31g
  • Protein: 24g

Ingredients

  • 1 12 ounce package jumbo pasta shells
  • 8 ounces diced mushrooms
  • 3 cloves minced garlic
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 pound ground turkey
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 10 ounce package frozen chopped spinach, thawed and drained
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 cups cottage cheese
  • 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 32 ounce jar spaghetti sauce
  • 8 ounces shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 F.
  2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add pasta shells and cook for 8-10 minutes. Drain and then set aside in warm water.
  3. Saute mushrooms, garlic and onions in a large skillet. Add ground turkey and cook until brown. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in spinach, parsley, cottage cheese, and Parmesan cheese.
  5. Stuff the cooked shells with this mixture and place in a 9x13 inch baking dish. Cover with spaghetti sauce and sprinkle top with mozzarella cheese.
  6. Cover with foil and bake for 30-45 minutes.
  7. Consume!
