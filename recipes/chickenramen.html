Creamy Blackened Chicken Ramen

Description

Chicken is a wonderfully tasty food! Ramen is also a scrumptious choice!

The edible parts of this dish will give you a lot of energy after you eat them!

Do not try to eat the bowl! That part is certainly inedible!

Serves 2

Nutritional Facts

  • Calories: 768
  • Fat: 44g
  • Carbs: 55g
  • Protein: 38g

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 skinless, boneless chicken breast
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Broth

  • 1 1/2 cloves garlic, minced or equivalent paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili flakes
  • 2 tablespoons marinara sauce
  • 3/4 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 1/4 cups chicken stock, divided
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 packages ramen noodles
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 sliced green onion
  • cilantro leaves

Steps

  1. Add flour, cayenne, garlic powder, half of salt, and half of black pepper to a shallow plate. Mix well.
  2. Season chicken breast with remaining salt and pepper. Cover chicken breast in seasoned flour and shake off excess.
  3. Heat oil in a pot over medium heat. Cook chicken breast in the pot, turning every 1 to 2 minutes. Cook until sides are blackened, then remove to a plate.
  4. Lower heat to medium-low. Add garlic, chili flakes, marinara sauce, smoked paprika, and oregano. Stir continuously. Stir in half of chicken stock and cream. Add remaining stock, increase heat to medium-high.
  5. Add ramen noodles. Cook in broth according to package instructions. Bring heat down to medium-low and simmer for a few minutes.
  6. Slice chicken. Ladle broth and noodles into shallow bowls. Lay sliced chicken pieces on top. Garnish with Parmesan, green onions, and cilantro.
  7. Serve and devour!
