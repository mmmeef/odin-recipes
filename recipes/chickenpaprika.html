Sour Cream Chicken Paprika

Description

A wonderful dish that exists and is made of food! It contains many different flavors!

This chicken can be eaten by all animals and has nutritional value! It is certainly a must try!

Serves 4

Nutritional Facts

  • Calories: 363
  • Fat: 23g
  • Carbs: 9g
  • Protein: 30g

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 1 cup chopped onion
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 8 ounces sour cream
  • 1 teaspoon all-purpose flour

Steps

  1. Gather all listed ingredients.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add chicken and pan-fry for 10 minutes. Remove chicken and set aside.
  3. Cook onion in the same skillet until translucent, but not brown. Season with paprika and salt.
  4. Pour chicken stock into the pan and bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce heat to a simmer. Mix sour cream with flour until smooth, then whisk into chicken stock.
  6. Place chicken in skillet. Simmer until chicken is heated through and sauce has thickened.
  7. Enjoy!
